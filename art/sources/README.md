# Art sources

Stills made outside this repo, which an art script starts from. Put each file here under the name below before running its script.

- `city-hall.png`: the PixelLab city hall still that `art/buildings/city_hall.py` animates (it also needs scipy). In the chat where it was made, it was the upload `pixellab-Isometric-view-of-a-rounded-wh-1790537814300.png`.
- `drone-port.png`: the PixelLab drone port still that `art/buildings/drone_port.py` checks against the city's plot and animates. It is the Pro result of PixelLab test 5 (Pro at 256 by 256, the city hall as the style image, seed 2106); a copy sits in `_extras/pixellab-tests/test5/pro/frame_00.png`, one folder up from the source.
- `lot-playground.png`: the PixelLab playground still that `art/lots/playground.py` fills to the plot's edge and animates. It is the Pro result of PixelLab test 6 (Pro at 256 by 128, the first frame of `lot-park.gif` as the style image, seed 2106); a copy sits in `_extras/pixellab-tests/test6/pro/frame_00.png`, one folder up from the source.
- `lot-sculpture-garden.png`: the PixelLab floating stones still that `art/lots/sculpture_garden.py` lays on the park lawn and animates. It is the Pro result of 7 October 2026 (Pro at 256 by 128, the first frame of `lot-park.gif` as the style image, seed 2106). Pro drew no ground under it: only the stones, plinths, stepping stones, benches and shrubs, on transparency.
- `lot-mini-golf.png`: the PixelLab lagoon course still that `art/lots/mini_golf.py` lays on the park lawn and animates. It is the Pro result of 7 October 2026 (Pro at 256 by 128, the first frame of `lot-pond.gif` as the style image, seed 2106). Pro drew no ground under it: only the course, the pond with its bridge, the lighthouse, the flags, the shrubs and a little windmill, on transparency.
- `props/`: the street props' PixelLab stills that `art/props/props.py` finishes, a front and a back for each. The fronts are Pixflux (isometric, high top-down, the city's 32 colors forced, seed 2106); the backs are views picked from a Pro Flash rotation of each front: `north` for the bus stop and the billboard, `north-east` for the vending machine, `north` for the bin. The bus stop, vending machine and bin come from PixelLab test 3 (`_extras/pixellab-tests/test3/`, one folder up from the source); the billboard was made on 7 October 2026 with the same recipe, and replaced the test's white one.
